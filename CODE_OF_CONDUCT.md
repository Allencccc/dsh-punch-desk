# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never post real session data, credentials, personal absolute paths, or private conversations.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab or the maintainer contact. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors. Reports are handled confidentially.
